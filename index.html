01 / About

A little about me

I'm Syed Saad Aamir — a frontend developer and UI/UX designer who cares about the small details that make a product feel effortless.

My curiosity about how websites work turned into a craft: designing interfaces in Figma, then turning them into fast, accessible and responsive experiences with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. I enjoy the whole path from first idea to polished product, and I'm always learning the next thing.
